# 🏥 Healthcare-Dashboard  

## 🎯 Business Problem  
Healthcare providers face challenges in:  
- 📈 Monitoring patient admissions & discharges effectively  
- 🩺 Tracking treatment outcomes & efficiency  
- 🏥 Identifying bottlenecks in hospital operations  
- 💡 Improving patient care with limited resources  

---

## 🎯 Goal  
The goal of this dashboard is to:  
- ⏱ Provide real-time visibility into healthcare data  
- 📊 Enable stakeholders to track key health indicators  
- 🧩 Support data-driven decisions for better outcomes & efficiency  

---

## 📊 Key Visuals (Dashboard Insights)  
- 👨‍⚕️ **Admissions & Discharges Trend** → patient inflow & outflow patterns  
- 🩺 **Treatment Success Rate** → measure effectiveness of treatments  
- 🏥 **Hospital Utilization** → bed occupancy, staff allocation, resources  
- 👥 **Demographics Breakdown** → insights by age, gender, region
